# Worked example: a 4-minute pre-meeting brief

An anonymized record of a real brief built with this skill. The client, people and figures are replaced with placeholders; the timings, structure and lessons are real. Use it as a reference for pacing and scene density.

- **Audience:** two partners, watching before an internal alignment meeting and ahead of a client readout.
- **Deliverable:** one 1920x1080 MP4 plus a contact sheet JPG.

## Inputs

- A locked script with an 8-cut list.
- The analysis write-up (`FINDINGS.md`) and the dashboard data export behind it.
- The project's decision notes.
- The latest client call transcript, used for the verbatim quote.

## Voice

- 567 written words became 615 spoken words and 3,860 characters.
- One take: 243.3 s. ElevenLabs billed fewer characters than the text length (check the `character-cost` header; it is what you pay).
- Pace: 152 wpm.
- Whisper small.en check: a clean read.

## Number decisions (reported to the user)

- **A share quoted "of revenue in August"** became "in our August numbers". The figure came from a longer-window analysis done in August, not from August revenue alone.
- **A travel-cost claim** was left off screen. It had been withdrawn on a later call.
- **Monthly counts with a partial last month** were labelled honestly on the axis ("Nov 1-17").
- **The quote** used the client's actual answer from the transcript, not the script's paraphrase.
- **"Over thirteen thousand entries"** in the voice; the exact 13,182 on screen.
- **A year-over-year pair** was labelled "last year / this year", not specific years, because the exact window was not certain.

## Measurements

- 1920x1080, 30 fps, 247.0 s, 45.5 MB (delivery quality). Rendered in under 2 minutes with 6 workers on a desktop-class Linux box.
- -14.0 LUFS integrated, -1.0 dBTP, LRA 3.6. No music. 11 SFX.
- The first silence end matched in the voice and the render (34.26 s), so sync was intact.
- 7 frames around each of the 8 cuts were checked: no flicker.

## What the audience said

They loved how it moved. The open issue was the runtime: 4:07 against a 2:50 target, caused by the length of the approved script. Count spoken words before the take.
